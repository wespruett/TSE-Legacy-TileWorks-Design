About

A Legacy Built in Tile

“The little details are what make the difference between a tile setter and a tile mason.” That was one of the first lessons Cameron learned from his father, a tile mason with more than 40 years in the trade. It's also the idea behind TSE Legacy Tileworks & Design. The name Legacy is intentional. So is TSE. TSE stands for Tile Setters Extraordinaire, the name Cameron's father gave his own tile business decades ago. Today, TSE Legacy Tileworks & Design carries that name forward, along with the knowledge, standards, and craftsmanship passed from father to son.

Cameron Ramsey

From Helper to Legacy Tile Craftsman

Cameron grew up around the tile trade and placed his first tile at just two years old. At 15, he earned his first paycheck working as his father's helper.

Years later, Cameron and his father moved to Colorado and went into business together. For roughly three years, Cameron apprenticed under his father, learning the trade from the ground up. He started with the fundamentals—preparation, mixing, cutting, grouting and cleaning—before progressing into layout, setting, and eventually leading installations of his own.

Over the following years, Cameron built more than 12 years of professional experience across residential remodeling, custom homes, and commercial installations.

His foundation comes from traditional tile masonry, including mud work, proper preparation, careful layout, and an understanding that a finished installation is only as good as what's underneath it. Today, he combines that old-school foundation with modern waterproofing systems, materials, and installation methods.

The tools and methods continue to evolve. The standard doesn't.

Discuss a Project

Preparation Is the Foundation

Structure, flatness, moisture management, and transitions determine whether the visible tile can perform correctly.

Layout Is Deliberate

Focal points, fixtures, corners, niches, openings, cuts, grout joints, and adjoining surfaces are considered before installation begins.

Communication Is Direct

Scope, exclusions, changes, material concerns, and schedule impacts remain clear throughout the project.

Finish Work Matters

Edges, sealant, cleaning, protection, punch work, and final review separates a craftsmanship from basic installers.

Custom tile, stone, shower, flooring, backsplash, fireplace, and design work completed with careful preparation and lasting attention to detail.
